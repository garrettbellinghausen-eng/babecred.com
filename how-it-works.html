How BabeCred Works

The math behind the system. It's rigged against you. Here's how to survive anyway.

The Two Rules

Rule #1: Deposits Decay

Good things you do lose value over time. She forgets. It's not personal — it's biology. Last week's flowers? Already fading.

Rule #2: Withdrawals Stick

Bad things recover slowly. Very slowly. She remembers that boys' weekend for weeks. The gaming session from last Tuesday? Still on the books.

How Deposits Work

Every deposit has a half-life — the number of days until it's worth half as much. Then half again. Then half again. Until it's basically zero.

See the problem? Flowers take 10 days to disappear. A boys' weekend takes 40 days. You need to bring flowers four times just to offset one weekend. And each round of flowers is decaying while you're bringing the next one.

Your Balance

Your balance is calculated in real-time: the sum of all your decaying deposits plus all your slowly-recovering withdrawals. It's a moving target that's always sinking unless you're actively depositing.

You are almost always negative. That's not a bug — it's the system working as designed. Relationships require constant maintenance. The default state is deficit.

How to Win

🎯 Strategy #1: Plan Ahead

Big withdrawal coming? (Boys' weekend, Final Four, golf trip) Start banking cred NOW. You need to overshoot because your deposits decay while you save. Scheduling future withdrawals shows you the projected impact.

📈 Strategy #2: Compound Interest

Small daily deposits beat one big gesture. Taking out the trash every day (+25 each time) keeps a steady flow of cred even as each one decays. Consistency > grand gestures.

⚡ Strategy #3: Unprompted > Asked

Did she ask you to do it? It still counts, but it's worth less. The same action done unprompted — because you just noticed it needed doing — is worth significantly more. She notices.

🚫 Strategy #4: Avoid Catastrophics

Forgetting an anniversary (-150, +1/day) takes 5 months to clear. No amount of flowers fixes that. Set up Babe Info (tap 👸) so the system warns you in advance. Prevention > recovery.

🏆 Strategy #5: Check the Leaderboard

If you're in the Top 5, you can golf. If you're in the Bottom 5, stay home and do the dishes. The leaderboard doesn't lie.
